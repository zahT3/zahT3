# Hi, I'm zahT3

I build practical systems at the intersection of AI agents, quantitative research, automation, and Web3.

My work is organized around one principle: repositories hold the durable context; models and agent clients are replaceable tools.

## Selected work

- [Falling Stars](https://github.com/zahT3/falling-stars) — an interactive 3D archive of 31,956 meteorite recoveries.
- [DeFiLlama Notion Sync](https://github.com/zahT3/defillama-notion-sync) — a small data pipeline that turns on-chain economics into a practical research signal.

## How I work

- Reproducible research over one-off answers.
- Tests and decision records over chat history.
- Private by default; public after a security and provenance review.

Most experiments begin in private repositories. The useful, reproducible parts graduate here when they are ready to share.
